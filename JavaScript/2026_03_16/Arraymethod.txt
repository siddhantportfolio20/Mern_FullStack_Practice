What are methods ?
Note:- methods and function are same , methods are aslo called as functions  

there are 4 arrays methods 
1> push() 
2> pop()
3> unshift
4> shift

_________________________________________________________________   
|Methods | Action        | where            | Returns            |
|________|_______________|__________________|____________________|
|push    | add           | end              | new length         |   
|pop     | remove        | end              | removed element    |
|unshift | add           | begining         | new length         |
|shift   | remove        | begining         | removed element    |
|________|_______________|__________________|____________________|

Methods are used for array modification

//Begining :- adding or removing element at the begining we use unshift & shift 
//End :- adding or removing element at the end we use push & pop
